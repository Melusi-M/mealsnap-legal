Delete Your MealSnap Account

Last updated: May 6, 2026

MealSnap, operated by Emmanqubic, gives you full control over your account and the data you create in the app. This page explains how to permanently delete your account and all associated data, what gets deleted, and what (if anything) is kept.

Option 1: Delete from inside the app (recommended)

If you still have MealSnap installed:

  1. Open MealSnap on your device.
  2. Sign in if you are signed out.
  3. Go to Settings (the gear icon on the Profile tab).
  4. Scroll to Delete Account — "Permanently delete your account and all data".
  5. Tap it and confirm Delete My Account in the prompt.

Your account, profile, meals, photos, and logs are permanently removed from our database immediately. Backups are purged within 30 days.

Option 2: Request deletion by email

If you have already uninstalled MealSnap or cannot sign in, send an email to support@mealsnap.app from the email address registered to your MealSnap account.

Subject: Delete my MealSnap account

Body: Please delete my MealSnap account and all associated data. The email address on the account is the one I'm sending from.

We will verify the request, delete the account within 7 business days, and email you to confirm. All backups containing your data are purged within 30 days of the request.

Delete some of your data without deleting your account

You don't have to delete the whole account to remove specific data. From inside the app you can:

  • Delete individual meals from your history (swipe or use the delete action on a meal entry).
  • Delete individual water entries from the water log.
  • Delete individual weight entries from the weight log.
  • Delete saved meals / favorites from the saved meals list.

For any partial deletion request that can't be completed in-app, email support@mealsnap.app describing what you want removed.

What gets deleted

When you delete your account, the following data is permanently removed:

ProfileEmail, name, age, gender, height, weight, target weight, activity level, calorie and macro goals, water goal, target date.
Meal logsAll meals, foods, calories, macros, meal types, timestamps.
Meal photosAll photos uploaded for AI food analysis, removed from cloud storage.
Saved mealsFavorites and saved meal templates.
Daily summariesAggregated daily nutrition totals.
Water logsAll water intake entries.
Weight logsAll weight entries and notes.
AuthenticationSign-in credentials and session tokens.
Push notification tokenDevice token used to send reminders.

What we keep, and why

DataWhy it is retainedRetention period
Anonymized crash reports and diagnostics Crash logs collected through Expo's built-in crash reporting are not linked to your account once it is deleted; they remain only as anonymous app stability data. Up to 90 days
Subscription / purchase records If you ever subscribed to MealSnap Premium, the purchase record is held by Google Play (Android) or Apple (iOS) and by RevenueCat for tax, refund, and accounting compliance. We do not store payment card details at any time. As required by Google, Apple, and applicable tax law (typically up to 7 years)
Records required by law If we are legally required to retain specific records (e.g. a fraud or law-enforcement request), we will retain only what the law requires. As required by law

Retention period

Your account and the data listed above are removed from our active database immediately on deletion. Encrypted database backups are rotated and fully purged within 30 days, after which no copy of your account data remains in our systems.

Need help?

If anything on this page is unclear, or you want a deletion confirmation in writing, email support@mealsnap.app. You can also review our Privacy Policy for the full picture of how we handle your data.
