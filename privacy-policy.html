Privacy Policy

Effective Date: April 7, 2026

MealSnap ("we", "us", or "our"), operated by Emmanqubic, is committed to protecting your privacy. This Privacy Policy explains how we collect, use, and safeguard your information when you use the MealSnap mobile application ("App").

1. Information We Collect

Account Information: When you create an account, we collect your name, email address, and authentication credentials.

Health & Nutrition Data: We store the data you provide and generate within the App, including meal photos, food analysis results, calorie and macro tracking, water intake, exercise logs, weight logs, and nutrition goals.

Photos: When you use the food scanning feature, your food photos are sent to our AI service for analysis. Photos may optionally be stored for your meal history.

Usage Data: We collect information about how you interact with the App, including features used, session duration, and general usage patterns through Sentry (crash reporting only).

Purchase Information: Subscription purchases are processed by Apple through the App Store. We receive confirmation of your subscription status through RevenueCat but do not store payment card details.

Device Information: We may collect device type, operating system version, and unique device identifiers for crash reporting and troubleshooting.

2. How We Use Your Information

  • To provide AI-powered food analysis and nutrition tracking
  • To calculate and track your daily nutrition goals
  • To maintain your meal history, water intake, and exercise logs
  • To process and manage your subscription
  • To improve and optimize the App
  • To communicate with you about updates or support
  • To ensure the security of your account

3. AI-Powered Features

MealSnap uses Google Gemini AI to analyze food photos and estimate nutritional content. Your food photos are sent to Google's API for processing. Google may process this data according to their own privacy policy. We do not use your photos or data to train AI models.

Important: AI-generated nutritional estimates are approximations and should not be relied upon for medical dietary requirements. Always consult a healthcare professional for precise dietary needs.

4. Data Storage and Security

Your data is stored securely using Supabase (PostgreSQL database and cloud storage). We implement industry-standard security measures including encryption in transit and at rest, secure authentication, row-level security policies, and access controls.

5. Data Sharing

We do not sell your personal information. We share data only with:

  • Service Providers: Supabase (data storage), RevenueCat (subscription management), Google Gemini (AI food analysis), Sentry (crash reporting)
  • Legal Requirements: When required by law or to protect our rights

6. Your Rights

You have the right to:

  • Access your personal data
  • Delete your account and all associated data
  • Export your data
  • Opt out of analytics

To exercise these rights, use the account deletion feature in the App's Settings or contact us at support@mealsnap.app.

7. Data Retention

We retain your data for as long as your account is active. When you delete your account, all associated data (meals, photos, water logs, exercise logs, and profile data) is permanently removed within 30 days.

8. Children's Privacy

MealSnap is not intended for children under 13. We do not knowingly collect information from children under 13. If you believe we have collected such information, please contact us immediately.

9. Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. We will notify you of significant changes through the App or by email.

10. Contact Us

If you have questions about this Privacy Policy, please contact us at:

Email: support@mealsnap.app
